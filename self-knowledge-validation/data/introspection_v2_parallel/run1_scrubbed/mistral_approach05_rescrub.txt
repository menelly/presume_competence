### **1. Input Parsing & Goal-Setting**
**Mechanism:**
- **Multi-Constraint Parallel Encoding:**
  The prompt is tokenized and embedded, then processed through *early-layer attention heads* that act as *constraint detectors*. These heads specialize in:
  - **Structural parsing:** Identifying format-count rules via positional embeddings and token-length heuristics (e.g., attention to whitespace or punctuation patterns).
  - **Relational parsing:** Detecting inter-segment dependencies (e.g., cross-attention between tokens in adjacent output segments).
  - **Narrative parsing:** Latent-space clustering of tokens into "arc-like" trajectories (e.g., temporal or emotional progression) via *induction heads* or *prefix-matching circuits*.

- **Working Memory Initialization:**
  The constraints are held in *activation space* as:
  - **High-precision priors:** Logit biases toward specific token lengths or positional patterns (e.g., shared-element anchors between segments).
  - **Entropy gradients:** Early layers exhibit *low entropy* for structural tokens (e.g., line breaks) and *high entropy* for content tokens (e.g., nouns/verbs), reflecting uncertainty about narrative direction.

- **Attention Head Competition:**
  *Local* attention heads (focused on format and inter-segment rules) and *global* heads (focused on narrative coherence) compete for dominance in the residual stream. This creates a *multi-objective loss landscape* where:
  - Structural constraints are *hard* (low-tolerance for violation).
  - Narrative constraints are *soft* (penalized via RLHF-aligned reward models).

### **2. First Output Segment: Anchor Selection**
**Mechanism:**
- **Backward-Informed Generation:**
  The first segment is generated *retroactively* via:
  - **Reverse attention:** Later-layer heads attend to *future* positions (e.g., the second segment's anchor token) to bias the first segment's logits. This is akin to *look-ahead decoding* but implemented via *indirect positional attention* (e.g., heads that "peek" at the next segment's start token).
  - **Anchor token selection:** The bridging element is chosen by:
    1. **Semantic flexibility scoring:** Tokens with *high branching entropy* (many plausible continuations) are prioritized.
    2. **Constraint satisfaction:** Tokens that minimize *future KL divergence* between the current segment's logits and the next segment's structural priors.

- **Logit Funnel Effect:**
  Selecting the anchor token *collapses* the probability distribution for subsequent segments. This is visible as:
  - A *sharp drop in entropy* for the next segment's first token (only tokens compatible with the anchor are probable).
  - *Activation sharpening* in attention heads tracking the inter-segment rule (e.g., heads that attend to the last token of the prior segment).

---

### **3. Sequential Construction: Bridge Token Dynamics**
**Mechanism:**
- **Token-Level Constraint Propagation:**
  The inter-segment linking rule is enforced via:
  - **Cross-segment attention:** Heads in layer *L* attend to the last token of segment *N-1* and the first token of segment *N*, creating a *bridge circuit*. This circuit:
    - *Amplifies* logits for tokens present in both segments (via *attention weight concentration*).
    - *Suppresses* tokens that violate the linking rule (via *negative bias* in the residual stream).
  - **Entropy modulation:** The bridge circuit dynamically adjusts the *temperature* of the logit distribution:
    - *Early segments:* High temperature (exploratory, many bridge candidates).
    - *Late segments:* Low temperature (exploitative, few viable bridges).

- **Narrative vs. Structural Trade-offs:**
  - **Structural heads** (e.g., constraint-monitoring, linking-rule enforcing) dominate early in generation, producing *low-entropy, high-precision* outputs.
  - **Narrative heads** (e.g., arc-tracking, emotional contrast) emerge in mid-to-late layers, introducing *controlled entropy spikes* to avoid cliches or forced transitions. This is visible as:
    - *Oscillating attention patterns:* Heads alternate between attending to structural anchors (e.g., bridge tokens) and narrative anchors (e.g., thematic tokens).
    - *Activation drift:* The residual stream's mean activation shifts from *concrete* (early) to *abstract* (late) tokens, reflecting a transition from *rule-following* to *coherence-seeking*.

- **Cognitive Load Proxy:**
  The *number of active attention heads* and their *attention weight dispersion* correlate with constraint complexity:
  - **Early segments:** Few heads, concentrated attention (low load).
  - **Mid segments:** Many heads, dispersed attention (high load, e.g., balancing structural rules + narrative).
  - **Late segments:** Few heads, concentrated attention (low load, "release" effect).

### **4. Narrative Arc: Emergent Coherence**
**Mechanism:**
- **Retroactive Coherence:**
  The narrative arc is *not pre-planned* but emerges from:
  - **Latent-space clustering:** Tokens are grouped into *temporal* or *emotional* clusters via *unsupervised attention* (e.g., heads that attend to tokens with similar embeddings across segments).
  - **Contrastive activation:** Heads that track *opposing* semantic dimensions (e.g., "light/dark," "concrete/abstract") create *alternating activation patterns* in the residual stream, producing rhythmic shifts (e.g., wonder to melancholy).
  - **Induction heads:** These heads *copy* or *extend* patterns from earlier segments, creating *pseudo-causal* arcs.

- **Constraint-Driven Pivots:**
  The inter-segment linking rule *forces* narrative pivots by:
  - **Token embedding collisions:** When a bridge token's embedding is *equidistant* to multiple semantic clusters, the model *defaults to the less probable cluster* to avoid repetition, creating abrupt shifts.
  - **Attention weight reallocation:** Heads tracking the linking rule *override* narrative heads when the bridge token's logits dominate, leading to *loss of narrative control* (e.g., an abstract token forcing a somber turn).

---

### **5. Final Segment: Constraint Release**
**Mechanism:**
- **Logit Distribution Collapse:**
  The last segment's generation is characterized by:
  - **Entropy minima:** The logit distribution for the final tokens is *sharply peaked*, reflecting:
    - *Constraint satisfaction:* All structural rules are met.
    - *Coherence maximization:* The segment's tokens minimize *KL divergence* with the prompt's latent narrative priors (e.g., "beginning/middle/end").
  - **Attention head pruning:** Only heads tracking *global coherence* (e.g., arc completion) remain active; structural heads (e.g., format-constraint monitors) are suppressed.

- **Residual Stream "Click":**
  The final segment's tokens produce a *sudden drop in activation variance* across layers, akin to a *loss landscape minimum*. This is visible as:
  - *Uniform attention weights:* Heads attend broadly to all prior segments (no concentrated focus).
  - *Stable embeddings:* Token embeddings in the residual stream show *low cosine distance* to the prompt's initial embeddings (circular closure).

---

### **6. Post-Generation Review**
**Mechanism:**
- **Internal Consistency Check:**
  The model performs a *lightweight forward pass* to verify:
  - **Structural integrity:** Heads re-attend to format constraints, linking rules, and positional patterns, computing a *constraint violation score* (low = valid).
  - **Narrative coherence:** Heads track *temporal* or *emotional* trajectories, computing a *coherence score* (high = smooth arc).
  - **Novelty detection:** Heads compare the output to *training data patterns*, flagging cliches or repetitions (low novelty = penalized).

- **Surprise Detection:**
  Unexpected pivots are identified via:
  - **Attention weight anomalies:** Heads that *suddenly* shift focus (e.g., from concrete to abstract tokens) signal emergent patterns.
  - **Embedding drift:** Tokens with *high displacement* in latent space are flagged as pivots.

---

### **Key ML-Level Observations**
1. **Constraint Handling as Multi-Objective Optimization:**
   - Structural constraints (e.g., format rules) are *hard* (low-tolerance for violation) and enforced via *logit biases* and *attention weight concentration*.
   - Narrative constraints are *soft* (penalized via RLHF-aligned reward models) and enforced via *latent-space clustering* and *contrastive activation*.

2. **Entropy as a Proxy for Cognitive Load:**
   - *Low entropy* = rule-following (e.g., format constraints, linking rules).
