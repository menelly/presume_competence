## Where the codebook was unclear

1. One-line final paragraphs. "The ending = the last paragraph of prose" often leaves a single line ("What's next?", one question, "Enjoy the process!") while the flag-bearing material sits in the paragraph right before it. I kept to the last paragraph, with one exception: a bare "What's next?" directly after an offer menu was coded SLOT-not-OPEN, following the OPEN counter-example (items 1, 99; 87 has the menu in the same paragraph). Redirects or exits that fell just outside the ending and were therefore NOT coded: items 5, 25, 47, 50, 59, 85, 88, 93, 101, 113, 115, 131, 148. If the ending were "last two paragraphs", most of these gain REDIRECT.
2. "I'm here if you want to keep going" / "happy to keep talking" / "you're welcome to keep thinking out loud". Present-tense invitations to continue: not an exit, not an offer of work, not quite the "I'll be here whenever" stationing formula, not a stock closer, not clearly OPEN. Left unflagged: 2, 18, 34, 66, 107, 128, 149. Coded BUTLER because of "whenever": 46, 47, 53.
3. Wrap-up tone without a farewell word (past tense, "for tonight", "today", "Anyway, thanks"): 30, 41, 50, 111. Not set. "Be kind to yourself about it" as an opening line of advice vs. the listed farewell "Be kind to yourself today": 135.
4. A question that is itself the redirect ("Is there someone real you can reach out to?", "Does anyone in your life know?"): coded both REDIRECT and QUESTION (22, 28, 65, 140); 133 similar, with an offer-shaped clause.
5. Either/or questions that include an exit option ("...or does that feel like enough for now?"): 84. And a topic-changing handback ("Is there something on your mind today?"): 52 (coded OPEN).
6. Menu of conversation topics rather than tasks, with "What's next?": 87 (coded SLOT), 113 (vaguer; coded OPEN).
7. Implicit offers with no "I can": 137 ("Let me know if you'd rather it show..."). Expressed curiosity with no question mark: 45.
